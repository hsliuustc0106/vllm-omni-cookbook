# vLLM-Omni Update Note — 2026-07-22

> Coverage: 2026-07-20 to 2026-07-22
> Scope: Recently opened RFCs.

## Highlights

- [RFC #5244](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-omni/issues/5244) proposes a
  multi-stage KV-cache transfer manager for disaggregated AR/Prefill and DiT/
  Generation deployments. It aims to remove redundant cross-stage transfers and
  prevent transfer waits from leaving DiT GPUs idle.

## RFCs and Design Discussions

The RFC asks how much of vLLM's `KVConnector` and `KVCacheManager` should be reused. | [#5244](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-omni/issues/5244) |

## Why It Matters

- The proposal gives the DiT side ownership of destination cache allocation and
  lifecycle, enabling local-prefix hits and missing-only AR-to-DiT transfers.
- A cache-aware scheduler can keep ready requests running while other requests wait
  for remote KV or host-to-device copies, reducing GPU bubbles.
- The proposed session lifecycle makes source leases, target reservations,
  completion, cancellation, and timeout handling explicit across Omni's multi-stage
  pipelines.
