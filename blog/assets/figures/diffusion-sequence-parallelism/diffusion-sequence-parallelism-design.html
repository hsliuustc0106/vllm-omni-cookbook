Sequence Parallelism: how Ulysses, Ring, Hybrid and AllGather-KV talk to each other

Diffusion transformers turn a 2K image or a long video into a huge token sequence. Sequence parallelism (SP) keeps that sequence sharded across GPUs so every rank runs the whole network on S/P tokens, and only the attention layers pay a communication tax. vLLM-Omni ships four strategies behind one plug-in interface.

0The shared skeleton: strategies are plug-ins around the attention kernel

Every SP mode reshards in and out of attention only. The rest of the DiT block — norms, MLPs, modulations, QKV/O projections — is sequence-independent, so it just runs on a shorter sequence. Two pieces make this work:

  • A declarative _sp_plan on the transformer: split inputs like hidden_states along dim 1 before forward(), re-gather after proj_out. No manual sharding code in the model body.
  • A ParallelAttentionStrategy selected per-forward in build_parallel_attention_strategy(): its pre_attention() / post_attention() hooks wrap whatever kernel backend (FA2/FA3/FA4, AITER, SDPA, TRT-LLM) the model picked. Strategy and kernel are orthogonal axes.

1Group topology: one SP group, factored two ways

sp_size = ulysses_degree × ring_degree. At init, set_seq_parallel_pg() splits each SP group into an Ulysses subgroup and a Ring subgroup. With the default use_ulysses_low=True, Ulysses groups are contiguous rank chunks and Ring groups are strided. Try the presets — this factorization is exactly what hybrid mode composes.

2Ulysses: trade your sequence shard for a head shard

Each rank enters attention with (B, S/P, H, D) — a slice of tokens, all heads. A single dist.all_to_all_single per tensor swaps the two: rank i sends its token slice to every peer and receives the slices that match its head subset, leaving (B, S, H/P, D) — the full sequence, a fraction of the heads. Attention is then an ordinary local kernel, and a reverse all-to-all restores the sequence shard. 4 collectives per layer: Q, K, V forward + output reverse.

What exactly crosses the wire

The all-to-all moves P pieces per rank, per tensor. Rank i's local (S/P, H) block is cut along heads into P groups; piece k — rank i's tokens × head group k — goes to rank k. Symmetrically, rank i receives from every rank r that rank's tokens × rank i's own head group. Stacking the P received pieces along the sequence axis is what turns (S/P, H) into (S, H/P). Text / joint stream

Joint (text-conditioning) Q/K/V are assumed replicated on all ranks. They are sliced by heads per Ulysses rank, concatenated to the image stream after the all-to-all, and the joint slice of the output is recombined with an all_gather over the head dimension on the way out.

Constraints & UAA mode

Strict mode fails fast unless H % P == 0 and shards divide evenly. The experimental ulysses_mode="advanced_uaa" (Ulysses Anything Attention) lifts both: it all-gathers the per-rank sequence lengths and feeds them to the all-to-all as output_split_sizes, and pads the head dim to the next multiple of P only inside the exchange, slicing the pad away after the reverse. That is what lets Z-Image-Turbo (30 heads) run ulysses_degree=4.

Best for: ≥1536px images and moderate sequences on fat interconnects (NVLink) — it moves the full Q/K/V/output volume twice per layer, but leaves a completely standard attention kernel in the middle and overlaps well with high bandwidth.

3Ring: Q never moves; K/V take a lap while you compute

No resharding at all. Each rank keeps its query shard and its K/V block; the kernel rotates K/V blocks around the ring with P2P isend/irecv (batch_isend_irecv), posting the transfer before calling attention so communication overlaps compute. After P steps every rank has attended to every K/V block; per-step partial outputs merge via online softmax (running out + log-sum-exp lse), fp32 by default. The output needs no post-processing — it is already sequence-sharded.

The online-softmax merge

m = max(lse, lseblock)
out = out·else−m + block_out·elseblock−m   ,   lse = m

Details that matter

  • Joint / text K/V never rotate — they are concatenated as a static prefix at step 0 only. joint_strategy="rear" + causal is rejected (the mask would hide the prefix).
  • Causal works by skipping steps where step > rank; diffusion is usually non-causal anyway.
  • Kernels: FA4 on Blackwell, FA3 elsewhere on NVIDIA, AITER on ROCm, PyTorch SDPA fallback (forced for fp32 and on hosts without flash wheels).
  • Fixed-shape P2P buffers — every rank in a ring group must hold the same K/V shape. That is the constraint hybrid mode has to respect.
Best for: videos and very long sequences. K/V travel one hop per step (not P−1), memory stays O(S/P), comm overlaps compute — but you pay P sequential block-wise attention calls, so short sequences drown in loop overhead.

4Hybrid Ulysses × Ring: all-to-all inner, ring outer

Set both degrees > 1 and the two compose over the factored groups from §1. The Ulysses all-to-all runs first inside each Ulysses subgroup — every rank now holds the full sequence for its head subset — and the ring then rotates those full-sequence K/V tensors across the strided Ring group. In the default layout (ranks 0-3, u=2, r=2): pairs {0,1} and {2,3} exchange heads, then ring pairs {0,2} and {1,3} rotate K/V.

Shape rule: Ring's P2P buffers are fixed-shape, so every rank in a ring group must end up with the same post-Ulysses S_global. UAA in hybrid mode validates this up front and raises instead of hanging the ring (ulysses.py:322-331). UAA also cannot pad K/V heads when Ring is active — padded zero heads would be repeated as real GQA/MQA heads.

5AllGather-KV: two all-gathers, zero reverse

The v1 minimalist. all_gather_into_tensor rebuilds the full K/V along the sequence dim on every rank; each rank then runs local attention of its S/P queries against the full keys and slices its attention mask / query-ranges to its own window. Q never moves, so there is no post-processing at all. Non-causal only — exactly diffusion's usual case — and mutually exclusive with Ulysses/Ring.
